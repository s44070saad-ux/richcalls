Questions

Asked by Almost Every Applicant

Is there any fee or investment?

None. Joining, training, certification and your Ringba dashboard are free, and you never buy stock or ads from us. If anyone asks you for money to join this program, it is not us.

How and when do I get paid?

Your qualified calls are totalled weekly from your dashboard and paid via Payoneer or Wise. Your per-call rate depends on your tier and campaign and is agreed with you in writing before you start a campaign.

What exactly makes a call “qualified”?

A genuine buyer, calling about a real product, who stays on the line past the campaign's minimum duration. Each campaign sets its own threshold — you will know yours before you publish anything, and your dashboard shows every call's status live.

Do I need a laptop or PC?

A laptop / PC is strongly preferred — and it is the honest answer if you want to earn a handsome amount. On a big screen you keep many buyer chats, your scripts and your Ringba dashboard open together, reply in seconds and manage far more ads. You can start on a smartphone, but phone-only partners handle less volume and are not first in line for the busiest campaigns. A basic laptop is enough; you do not need an expensive one.

Do I need sales experience?

No. Training gives you the ad templates, chat scripts and price answers, and most buyers simply want details and a price. What we screen for is clear written English, fast replies and honesty — the rest is teachable.

Can I do this alongside a job or studies?

Yes — many partners do. UK and US shopping hours fall in the South Asian evening and night. What matters is showing up consistently in those hours; partners who disappear mid-week lose their tier.

What gets an account closed?

Fake or self-made calls, misleading ads, sharing your dashboard login, or breaking the compliance rules you certified on. Companies audit call quality — one dishonest partner can cost every partner their campaign.

Home Services FAQ

        
Questions About the Trades Campaign

      

      

        
Is Home Services live right now?
Yes — Home Services is our primary, preferred campaign, live now, and it is the one we onboard partners to first. Furniture (beds & sofas) is the secondary campaign. Apply for the trade you want, complete its training and certification, and your areas are assigned against contractor coverage.

        
Do I need trade experience?
No — you need selling experience and trainability. Each trade has a playbook: the common jobs, the qualifying questions, typical price ranges and the words homeowners use. You certify on one trade before you take its calls.

        
What makes a home services call qualified?
A real homeowner, inside the contractor's service area, asking about a job in the right trade, who stays on the call past the campaign's duration threshold. Renters asking for work only a landlord can authorise, and out-of-area calls, do not qualify — your playbook teaches you to spot both in chat before the call.

        
How is it paid compared to furniture?
The same way: per qualified call, at a rate set by your tier and the trade campaign, agreed in writing before you start, totalled into the same weekly Payoneer or Wise payout. Urgent trades usually carry stronger rates than quieter work — your campaign sheet shows the exact rate before you commit.

        
Can I sell furniture and home services together?
Yes. One dashboard, one tier, one payout. Most partners start with a Home Services trade — it is our primary campaign — and add furniture as a secondary campaign if they want extra volume.

        
What hours do home services partners work?
US hours. Homeowners call when something breaks — mornings, evenings and weekends in the contractor's area. From South Asia that means late nights; the application asks your available hours honestly, and campaigns are assigned to match.
